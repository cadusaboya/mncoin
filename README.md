# Summary

Orebit is a blockchain-powered platform revolutionizing access to the manganese mining industry through a Mining-as-a-Service (MaaS) model. With one legally licensed mine in Marabá, Pará, Brazil — fully operational — and the goal to evolve into a scalable, tokenized ecosystem for real-world ore production.

Instead of just investing in a mining operation, Orebit allows users to stake $ORE tokens and receive manganese rewards proportional to their contribution, with the ability to unstake and exit anytime. Backed by real production and supported by a growing pipeline of new mines, Orebit combines transparency, on-chain reporting, and real asset exposure for both retail and institutional investors.

With over 3 million metric tons of high-quality manganese ore in estimated resources at Eagle — and an exploration target of up to 10 million tons across the broader 3,000-hectare site — Orebit aims to unlock the full value of manganese through horizontal growth (new mines) and vertical integration (refinery operations).

This documentation outlines Orebit’s business model, token mechanics, growth roadmap, and how to participate in this real-world asset platform.
