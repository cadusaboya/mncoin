# Geological Composition

The Eagle deposit consists of two primary ore bodies identified through extensive geological mapping and exploratory drilling. The site contains a combination of massive manganese ore blocks and detritic manganese, providing high-quality ore suitable for international markets.

### **Ore Characteristics**

* **Massive Manganese Ore**: Solid, dark gray, compact, and high-purity ore found in large blocks. It contains pegmatoid veins with quartz and feldspar, occasionally with biotite.
* **Detritic Manganese Ore**: A mixture of manganese pisolites, blocks, and iron concretions within a clay-silt matrix.
* **Banded Manganese Ore**: Characterized by alternating layers of manganese and feldspathic minerals, showing high recovery rates and Mn content ranging from 44% to 52%.
