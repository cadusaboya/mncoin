# Resources

The initial exploratory phase identified estimated resources of 3 million metric tons (MT) with Mn grades between 44% and 52%. The broader 3,000-hectare Eagle site presents an exploration target of up to 10 million MT, subject to further drilling. Additional geological studies are planned on unexplored areas to potentially find more manganese occurrences. At the current mining rate of 5,000 MT per month, the estimated resources represent a mine life of about 50 years.

{% hint style="info" %}
These figures are estimates from initial exploration work. They are not reserves, which require a formal feasibility study under recognized reporting standards (e.g. JORC, NI 43-101).
{% endhint %}

## Current Mining Operations

* Exploratory drilling confirmed significant manganese occurrences; ore-rich zones have been mapped.
* Trenching and sampling demonstrated consistent manganese mineralization, with detrital ore overlying primary manganese bodies.
* The mine is legally authorized to extract 60,000 MT per year.
* Once production begins, our legal team will initiate the process to increase the permitted volume to 120,000 MT per year.
* Monthly mining capacity may also be expanded further as operations scale.

## Expansion Plans

The project aims to increase production capacity through additional exploration, resource quantification, and process optimization. Future drilling campaigns and investment in advanced processing technologies will support higher extraction rates and ensure long-term sustainability.
