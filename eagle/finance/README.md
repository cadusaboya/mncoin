# Finance

This section details the estimated economics of the Eagle mine at its current licensed capacity of 60,000 metric tons (MT) of manganese ore per year (5,000 MT per month).

## Annual Summary

| Metric                              | Monthly (USD) | Annual (USD)       |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| Revenue                             | $945,000.00   | $11,340,000.00     |
| Logistics                           | $304,200.00   | $3,650,400.00      |
| Taxes and regulatory costs          | $67,887.50    | $814,650.00        |
| Operational costs                   | $103,672.73   | $1,244,072.76      |
| **Estimated profit**                | **$469,239.77** | **$5,630,877.24** |

## Profit Distribution

Following the [Orebit business model](../../orebit-fundamentals/ecosystem.md), the estimated profit from Eagle is split as follows:

| Recipient                          | Share | Annual (USD)      |
| ---------------------------------- | ----- | ----------------- |
| $ORE stakers in the Eagle mine pool | 80%   | $4,504,701.79     |
| Orebit (company)                    | 20%   | $1,126,175.45     |

## Assumptions

* **Volume:** 5,000 MT per month, the maximum allowed under Eagle's current permit of 60,000 MT per year.
* **Price:** $4.50 per 1% Mn grade per MT, applied to a 42% grade ($189.00 per MT).
* **Exchange rate:** BRL/USD at 5.72.
* **Taxes:** Includes CFEM, the state mineral tax and federal taxes on revenue. Figures are before corporate income tax.
* **Source:** Internal estimates based on the founders' previous operation of the Eagle mine. Actual results depend on ramp-up time, manganese prices, ore grade and logistics.
