### The Company

At the top of the pyramid sits Orebit, the company and orchestrator of the entire system. Orebit is responsible for platform growth, managing partnerships, legal compliance, the launch of new mine pools, and the long-term vision of vertical integration. To maintain operations and fund future growth, Orebit retains a portion of the profits generated across all mine pools.

### The Engine

Beneath Orebit are the mines — real, operational assets like Eagle, which form the engine of value creation. Each mine is equipped to extract and process manganese ore, with a portion of the extracted material retained locally to ensure the mine can sustain its own operations, cover on-site costs, and remain productive long-term. The remaining output feeds back into the staking economy, linking physical production with digital rewards.

### The Foundation

At the foundation of the pyramid is $ORE, the token that powers the entire ecosystem. By staking $ORE into a mine pool, token holders gain proportional access to the mine's production rewards. As their ore is sold, stakers reap the manganese-based profits in USDC, creating a direct connection between blockchain participation and real-world economic output.
