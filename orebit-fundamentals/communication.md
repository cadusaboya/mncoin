# Communication

We strongly value keeping our holders informed and engaged. Transparency is a core part of our project, and we are committed to providing regular, detailed updates on all aspects of our operations.

### Bi-Weekly Updates

We will document and release updates every two weeks, covering the latest developments in our mining operations, token management, and financial performance. Every payment and decision taken off-chain will be thoroughly documented and made available to the public. We aim to bridge the gap between traditional mining operations and blockchain transparency, ensuring that our community is always informed about critical operational decisions.

### On-Chain Fund Management

All project funds will remain on-chain in publicly known wallets. Every withdrawal or transfer of funds will be communicated promptly through our official channels.

By maintaining this level of openness, Orebit ensures that every investor and holder is kept in the loop, reinforcing trust and community engagement as the project grows.
