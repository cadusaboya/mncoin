# FAQ

## What is Eagle? Is it legal?

Eagle is a fully operational manganese mine located in Marabá, Pará, Brazil, deep within the Amazon region. It is owned by the Orebit founders and will serve as the first mine pool in the Orebit ecosystem.

Eagle is not a speculative asset — it's a licensed and producing site, already equipped and allowed to extract and sell up to 5,000 metric tons of manganese per month. It will be the launchpad for our model, allowing us to prove the viability of tokenizing real-world mining operations.

## What about regulatory compliance?

We have assembled a specialized legal team to ensure that all Orebit activities are fully compliant with both Brazilian and international law. Every stage of the project — from mining to tax structure — is being developed with full legal oversight to ensure maximum transparency and legitimacy.

## Who is the team?

Orebit is led by a fully doxxed, multidisciplinary team of founders, miners, legal experts, blockchain developers, and environmental consultants.

Rather than raising funds for a distant idea, we’re using our own mine as a proof of concept to back what we believe in. This personal commitment reflects our long-term vision of building a sustainable, real-world crypto infrastructure — starting from the ground up, literally.
